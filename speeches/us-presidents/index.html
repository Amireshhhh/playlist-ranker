How to read the proof

Every test treats a speech as the unit, so a refrain repeated many times counts as one piece of evidence. p is the chance of a result this extreme if the pattern were noise. q is p corrected (Benjamini-Hochberg) for the other tests in the same family. Held means q < 0.05; Partly means p < 0.05 that does not survive the correction. Contractions are expanded, so “I'm” counts as “I am”. With n eras the smallest possible exact p for an era trend is 2/n!.

Do eras have a fingerprint?

True era vs predicted era

Function words: 63% correct vs 16% by chance (permutation p 0.010). Without pronouns: 60%. Content words: 63%. 93% of guesses land within one era. Leave-one-song-out nearest centroid, Burrows' Delta.
