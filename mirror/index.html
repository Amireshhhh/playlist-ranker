Mirror

The same measures the Atlas uses on artists, run on your own writing: journal entries, essays, letters, posts. Watch your voice change over time: how often you write “I” versus “we”, how varied your words are, what you keep coming back to.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or saved.

Add text. Choose several .txt or .md files (one per entry; names that start with a date such as 2024-03-01 are sorted by it), or paste below with a line containing only --- between entries.

Who you write about

Per 100 words in each entry. “I” = I, me, my, mine, myself.

Word variety

Moving-average type-token ratio over 50-word windows, which does not depend on length. Blank under 50 words.

Your most used words

Function words removed.

Words that rose and fell

Rate per 1,000 words in your later half vs your earlier half (words used 5+ times).

Entry by entry

Method: tokens are letter sequences with contractions expanded (“I'm” → “I am”). The trend test is Spearman ρ between entry order and the entry's rate, with a permutation p over 10,000 shuffles of the order. Nothing here is a diagnosis of anything.
