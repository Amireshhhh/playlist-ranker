Method and limits

  • Catalogue: MusicBrainz studio albums (no live, compilation or bootleg albums), at most 20 per artist, the most common official tracklist of each. Lyrics: LRCLIB. Songs with no lyrics found are listed on each artist page.
  • Lyrics are read in memory during the build and never stored or published; this page holds word counts and scores only.
  • Lyrics transcriptions differ: a chorus written once instead of four times lowers repetition. Measures that ignore repeated lines are marked.
  • Theme scores use fixed word lists (water, sky & cosmos, light, darkness, fire, earth & nature, life & growth, death & pain, mind & soul, love, time, conflict); other lists would give other numbers.
  • Rankings show 95% intervals: artists whose intervals overlap are not reliably different.
