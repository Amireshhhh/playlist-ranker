How to read the proof

Every test treats a song as the unit, so a chorus repeated many times counts as one piece of evidence. p is the chance of a result this extreme if the pattern were noise. q is p corrected (Benjamini-Hochberg) for the other tests in the same family. Held means q < 0.05; Partly means p < 0.05 that does not survive the correction. Contractions are expanded, so “I'm” counts as “I am”. With n albums the smallest possible exact p for an album trend is 2/n!.

Do albums have a fingerprint?

True album vs predicted album

Function words: 11% correct vs 6% by chance (permutation p 0.040). Without pronouns: 11%. Content words: 6%. 25% of guesses land within one album. Leave-one-song-out nearest centroid, Burrows' Delta.

Mean lyric similarity between albums

TF-IDF cosine between songs, averaged by album pair. Songs drift apart with time: Mantel ρ -0.038, p 0.046.

Map of all songs

Classical MDS (PCoA) of TF-IDF cosine distance; lines join each song to its nearest neighbour. The two axes hold 3% of the variance, so trust the links more than the layout.

Words that keep company

A link joins two words seen within 3 consecutive lines at least 3 times, in at least 2 songs, and at least 3× more often than with lines shuffled (30 shuffles). 259 links from 3400 pairs tested; 8 survive Bonferroni correction (orange). Language laws

Zipf: rank vs frequency

Log-log. Slope -1.18 (dotted: ideal −1), R² 0.991. Discrete power-law MLE α 1.55.

Heaps: vocabulary growth

Band: 95% range of 1,000 shuffled song orders. Heaps β 0.68. Career order vs shuffles: p <0.001 (max-deviation test). A significant dip below the band would mean the writer was running out of new words.
